# References: Personal Finance and Investment

1. [Personal Finance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Personal_finance) - Wikipedia - Overview of financial planning for individuals including budgeting, saving, investing, insurance, and retirement planning fundamentals.

2. [Compound Interest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Compound_interest) - Wikipedia - Explains how interest earned on both principal and previously earned interest causes money to grow exponentially over time, the foundation of long-term investing.

3. [Credit Score](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Credit_score) - Wikipedia - Covers how credit scores are calculated, what factors affect them, why they matter for borrowing costs, and how to build and maintain good credit.

4. The Intelligent Investor (Revised Edition) - Benjamin Graham - Harper Business - Classic investment book teaching the principles of value investing, risk management, and the importance of disciplined long-term thinking for all investors.

5. A Random Walk Down Wall Street (13th Edition) - Burton Malkiel - W.W. Norton - Influential book making the case for index fund investing, explaining why most people are better off with low-cost diversified portfolios than picking individual stocks.

6. [Saving and Investing](https://www.econlib.org/library/Enc/Saving.html) - Concise Encyclopedia of Economics - Expert explanation of why saving matters for individuals and the economy, the relationship between saving and investment, and the role of interest rates.

7. [Consumer Financial Protection Bureau](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/consumer-tools/) - CFPB - Government resource with practical tools and guides for managing personal finances including budgeting, credit, debt, and student loans.

8. [CFPB Guide to Credit Reports and Scores](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/consumer-tools/credit-reports-and-scores/) - CFPB - Official government guide explaining how to check your credit report, understand your score, and dispute errors that could affect your financial life.

9. [30-Year Fixed Rate Mortgage Average](https://fred.stlouisfed.org/series/MORTGAGE30US) - Federal Reserve Economic Data (FRED) - Historical mortgage rate data showing how interest rates affect the cost of homeownership, the largest financial decision most people make.

10. [S&P 500 Index](https://fred.stlouisfed.org/series/SP500) - Federal Reserve Economic Data (FRED) - Long-term stock market data illustrating historical returns, volatility, and the power of compound growth for patient long-term investors.
