# References: Fiscal and Monetary Policy

1. [Fiscal Policy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fiscal_policy) - Wikipedia - Explains how governments use taxation and spending to influence the economy, covering expansionary and contractionary fiscal policy and automatic stabilizers.

2. [Monetary Policy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monetary_policy) - Wikipedia - Covers how central banks use interest rates and open market operations to control inflation and stabilize the economy through the money supply.

3. [Phillips Curve](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phillips_curve) - Wikipedia - Describes the observed trade-off between inflation and unemployment, its history, the expectations-augmented version, and its role in policy debates.

4. Macroeconomics (11th Edition) - N. Gregory Mankiw - Worth Publishers - Comprehensive textbook treatment of fiscal and monetary policy tools, their effectiveness, policy lags, and the debate between activist and rules-based approaches.

5. The Return of Depression Economics and the Crisis of 2008 - Paul Krugman - W.W. Norton - Nobel laureate's accessible analysis of how fiscal and monetary policy responded to financial crises, with lessons for understanding policy trade-offs.

6. [Fiscal Policy](https://www.econlib.org/library/Enc/FiscalPolicy.html) - Concise Encyclopedia of Economics - Expert analysis of government taxing and spending decisions, the multiplier effect, crowding out, and the effectiveness of fiscal stimulus.

7. [Monetary Policy](https://www.econlib.org/library/Enc/MonetaryPolicy.html) - Concise Encyclopedia of Economics - Covers how the Federal Reserve conducts monetary policy, the transmission mechanism, and debates about rules versus discretion in central banking.

8. [Federal Debt: Total Public Debt](https://fred.stlouisfed.org/series/GFDEBTN) - Federal Reserve Economic Data (FRED) - Data on the total U.S. national debt, essential for understanding the long-term consequences of fiscal policy and deficit spending.

9. [Federal Reserve Monetary Policy Releases](https://www.federalreserve.gov/monetarypolicy.htm) - Board of Governors of the Federal Reserve System - Official statements, meeting minutes, and reports on how the Federal Reserve makes monetary policy decisions.

10. [Debt to GDP Ratio](https://fred.stlouisfed.org/series/GFDEGDQ188S) - Federal Reserve Economic Data (FRED) - National debt as a percentage of GDP, the most meaningful measure of whether government borrowing is sustainable relative to the economy's size.
