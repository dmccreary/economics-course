# References: Measuring the Economy

1. [Gross Domestic Product](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gross_domestic_product) - Wikipedia - Explains how GDP is calculated using expenditure, income, and production approaches, plus the distinction between nominal and real GDP.

2. [Business Cycle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Business_cycle) - Wikipedia - Covers the recurring pattern of expansion and contraction in economic activity, including peaks, troughs, recessions, and recoveries.

3. [Aggregate Demand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aggregate_demand) - Wikipedia - Describes the total demand for goods and services in an economy at various price levels, and the factors that shift the aggregate demand curve.

4. Macroeconomics (11th Edition) - N. Gregory Mankiw - Worth Publishers - Leading macroeconomics textbook with thorough coverage of GDP measurement, business cycles, and the aggregate demand-aggregate supply model.

5. GDP: A Brief but Affectionate History - Diane Coyle - Princeton University Press - Engaging short book on how GDP was invented, what it measures well, what it misses, and why it matters for economic policy.

6. [Gross Domestic Product](https://www.econlib.org/library/Enc/GrossDomesticProduct.html) - Concise Encyclopedia of Economics - Expert explanation of GDP concepts, measurement methods, and the limitations of GDP as a measure of economic well-being.

7. [Real Gross Domestic Product](https://fred.stlouisfed.org/series/GDPC1) - Federal Reserve Economic Data (FRED) - Inflation-adjusted GDP data for the United States, showing the actual growth of economic output over time.

8. [Real GDP Per Capita](https://fred.stlouisfed.org/series/A939RX0Q048SBEA) - Federal Reserve Economic Data (FRED) - GDP divided by population, the best single measure of average living standards and how they change over time.

9. [GDP Growth Rate](https://fred.stlouisfed.org/series/A191RL1Q225SBEA) - Federal Reserve Economic Data (FRED) - Quarterly GDP growth rates showing the pace of economic expansion and contraction, essential for identifying business cycle phases.

10. [World GDP Data](https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/NY.GDP.MKTP.CD) - World Bank - International GDP data allowing comparison of economic output across countries and tracking of global economic growth trends.
